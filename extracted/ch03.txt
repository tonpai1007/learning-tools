Equilibrium
• Equilibrium
– Object does not move or moves with constant velocity → No change in motion
– Motion of an object is time-independent
• Under equilibrium, relation between force and motion of an object
follows Newton’s 1st law of motion
– For an object, if there is no net force acting upon, it will be in equilibrium
– That is: Under equilibrium condition, The resultant of all forces is zero
